# Socket Technical Enablement Exercise

Public working repository for a Socket Technical Enablement Lead interview exercise.

## Organization

- [research/](research/README.md): source index, attributed public user reports, assumptions and evidence trail. Public claims are **not** automatically verified facts.
- [project/](project/README.md): lab notes, friction log, training design, talk track and readiness assessment.

Research and deliverables live in separate folders **within the same repository**. This keeps evidence and decisions traceable without mixing unfiltered notes into finished lessons.

## Evidence rules

1. **Official documentation**: what Socket publicly claims or instructs; confirm against the actual environment when possible.
2. **Public report**: what an identified author alleges or experiences; preserve a link and its scope, and do not generalize to all customers.
3. **Assumption / hypothesis**: an explicit working premise made to design or prioritize training.
4. **Observed / reproduced**: firsthand evidence from our own lab, including environment/version, expected versus actual behavior.
5. **Training decision**: rationale linking back to documented evidence or an explicitly labeled assumption.

A claim can inform a hypothetical training scenario without being adopted as truth. Never teach unverified claims as product behavior. Do not post credentials, enterprise tokens, personal account details, or real security-sensitive configuration in this public repository.
